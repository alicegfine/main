How to use

Build and edit the org chart here. Changes save automatically, and everyone with the link sees the same live copy.

Overview

The tabs across the top are versions of the org. Edit the chart you're on in the left panel.

Editing people

Click Edit people to open the roster. Each person is a card.

  • Add someone: Click + Add person, then fill in Name and Title. Leave the name blank for an open role.
  • Category: Sets the card's color (Executive / Director / Manager / IC).
  • Status: Filled, or Hiring now / soon / later for a planned role.
  • Reports to: Type a manager's name and pick them to draw the reporting line.
  • Manager checkbox: Makes a person available in others' "Reports to" list.
  • Trash icon: Remove someone — their reports move up to that person's manager.

Changing who reports to whom

  • One person: Change their Reports to field in Edit people.
  • Several at once: In Reassign reports in the left sidebar, pick a new manager, tick who should report to them, and click Reassign selected.
  • Co-leaders: When two people are the same level (matching Category), open the report's card in Edit people and tick Co-leader — they'll sit side by side at the same tier, keeping the line between them.

Versions

  • New version: Click + at the end of the tabs to copy the tab you're on for editing.
  • Rename: Double-click a tab.
  • Reorder: Drag a tab left or right.
  • Delete: Click the × on a tab (there's an undo button in case you click by accident).
  • Compare: Click Compare to see versions side by side.
  • Private: Tick Private in the left panel to keep a version only in your browser (marked 🔒). Checking it removes that version from everyone else's view; unchecking shares it back. It's tied to this browser, so it won't show up on your other devices.

Appearance

  • Layout (remembered per version): Stacked teams for a full org on a slide, or Wide rows for a small/simple org.
  • Chart background: Type your slide's hex color so screenshots and exports match your deck.
  • Title & logo: Add an optional chart title and upload a logo to pin in any corner — both appear on screen and in every export.

Exporting to a deck

Click Export ▾ → PNG (picture), SVG (editable), or Copy image (paste straight into a slide).

Share a read-only view

Use Copy view-only link (bottom of the left panel) to give your team a live, read-only view of the current org at a separate URL — they can see it but not edit it, and it updates automatically as you make changes.
