How to use

Build and edit the org chart here. Changes save automatically, and everyone with the link sees the same live copy.

The big picture

  • The tabs across the top are versions of the org (e.g. Current org, Future hires). Click one to switch — the chart updates instantly.
  • The left panel is where you edit. The chart on the right is what you export into slides.

Editing people

Click Edit people to open the roster. Each person is a card.

  • Add someone: click + Add person, then fill in Name and Title. Leave the name blank for an open role.
  • Category sets the card's color (Executive / Director / Manager / IC).
  • Status is Filled, or Hiring now / soon / later for a planned role (shown as a dashed card with a colored tag).
  • Reports to — type a manager's name and pick them to draw the reporting line. Leave as "Top of chart" for the top role.
  • Manager checkbox makes a person available in others' "Reports to" list. (Anyone who already has reports is a manager automatically.)
  • Remove with the trash icon — their reports move up to that person's manager.

Changing who reports to whom

  • One person: change their Reports to field in Edit people.
  • Several at once: in Reassign reports, pick a new manager, tick who should report to them, and click Reassign selected.

Versions (the tabs)

  • New version: click + at the end of the tabs — it copies the tab you're on, so you can try what-ifs safely.
  • Rename: double-click a tab. Reorder: drag a tab left or right.
  • Delete: click the × on a tab. Deleted by accident? Click Undo in the message at the bottom.
  • Compare: click Compare to see versions side by side.

Appearance

  • Layout (remembered per version): Stacked teams for a full org on a slide, or Wide rows for a small/simple org.
  • Chart background — type your slide's hex color (e.g. #ecefff) so screenshots and exports match your deck.

Getting it into a deck

Click Export ▾ → PNG (picture), SVG (editable), or Copy image (paste straight into a slide). You can also just screenshot the chart.
